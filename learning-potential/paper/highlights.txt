Highlights

- Models that solve no multi-turn task learn little except by fine-tuning.
- Fine-tuning on expert tool calls teaches every model to call tools when none fits.
- A reasoning model was harmed by expert targets, not by its own verified successes.
- Above that level, benchmark gains are small, detectable only over pooled questions.
- Under LoRA, own successes gained no less on BFCL multi-turn than expert answers.
