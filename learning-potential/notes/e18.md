# E18: the final BFCL exam

Nov 20–21, 2026

*First written as a working page; this note is the source of truth from 21 Nov 2026 and adds the results. It examines the final state of every run in [E15](e15.md) (notes), [E16](e16.md) (LoRA) and [E17](e17.md) (retrieval).*

## Goal

The probes of E15 to E17 measure learning on tasks from our own practice pool. E18 asks whether that learning shows on the official benchmark: every run's final state (task 1,200) takes the full BFCL v4 exam, the same as E14's floors, and is compared with that floor. The learning curve stays with the probes (BFCL at every snapshot would cost about 12 times as much); E18 is one exam per run.

## Protocol

| Part | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Exam | BFCL v4, all categories, bfcl-eval 2026.3.23, each model through its own handler and vLLM, as E14's floors ([deploy/gcp/run_bfcl_arms.sh](../src/deploy/gcp/run_bfcl_arms.sh)) |
| Notes (E15) | The final notes (notes-1200.txt) in a system message of every question, as in practice; merged into an existing system message where there is one |
| LoRA (E16) | The final adapter (adapter-1200) merged into the weights ([lab/e18/merge.py](../src/lab/e18/merge.py)), examined like a floor |
| Retrieval (E17) | Examples retrieved from the final memory (memory-1200.jsonl) for every question, with the run's token budget, in a system message |
| Floor | E14's frozen result for each model |
| Skipped | Runs that learnt nothing have the floor as their final state: E16 and E17 judging for Llama, Falcon and Gemma (no passed task, so no adapter and an empty memory) |

31 exams: the three middle models × 6 runs, Qwen3-1.7B's 600-token retrieval run, and the three zero-floor models × 4 runs (two of notes, LoRA teaching, retrieval teaching). Results: `$BUCKET/bfcl/e18/<exp>-<model>-<run>.json`, in E14's format.

## Findings

1. **The probe ranking holds on the official exam, with smaller numbers.** On multi-turn base (the category the practice pool was built for), LoRA gives the largest gains (+2 to +11.5 for five models), retrieval helps the middle models (+3.5 to +5.5), notes move nothing (at most ±2). Probe gains of 10–60 points shrink to 2–11.5 on BFCL, whose tasks are harder and written by others.
2. **Only LoRA lifts the zero-floor models off zero** on multi-turn base: Llama 8, Falcon 3, Gemma 11.5.
3. **LoRA teaching breaks Qwen3-1.7B:** −9 on multi-turn base and −12.5 overall (live −30.5, non-live −26.1). Its judging adapter, trained on its own successes with its reasoning, is the best result for that model (+5, overall +1.1).
4. **Overall scores hide the learning.** Multi-turn base is one small slice of BFCL's overall score, and the methods move other categories too: LoRA drops memory to near 0 and cuts Qwen3.5's overall by 6.6–6.7; notes swing single-turn non-live (+10 to +20 for LFM2.5 and Qwen3.5, −20 to −25 for Llama). Reported changes should be per category, with multi-turn base as the target.
5. **Retrieval has the fewest side effects;** every non-target category moves by less than 2 points, except Qwen3-1.7B's memory (+2.6 to +5.8).

*E19 added 15 more exams with the same pipeline (seeds 16 and 17 of six LoRA runs, and teaching with the reasoning kept), stored beside these as `e19-<model>-<run>.json`; their results are in [E19](e19.md).*

## Statistical check (E20)

Tested in [E20](e20.md) (paired McNemar tests on the 200 multi-turn base questions, Holm-corrected, E19's exams included): of 46 exams, only LoRA teaching of Llama and Gemma (+8 to +12.5, every seed) and two of Qwen3-1.7B's reasoning-kept exams (−8.5, −9) differ significantly from the floor. LoRA beats retrieval on BFCL only for Llama and Gemma; for LFM2.5 and Qwen3.5 the two cannot be told apart. So finding 1 holds on our tasks for every model, but on BFCL only below the floor. The side effects in finding 4 are significant (Qwen3-1.7B teaching: live and non-live; LoRA teaching: memory; notes: non-live).

## What happened along the way

- **No C++ compiler (20 Nov):** the newer vLLM compiles kernels at start-up and fresh VMs had no g++ (E14's VMs had it). The launcher installs it, after refreshing the package index and waiting for the boot-time updates (a stale index left it uninstallable on a restarted VM).
- **Duplicate answers (21 Nov):** a launch cut off by a local time limit was started again, so two launchers on one VM appended answers to the same files, and bfcl refused to score them. Fixed with one launcher per VM (a lock) and [lab/e18/dedupe.py](../src/lab/e18/dedupe.py), which keeps the first answer to each question before scoring. All 31 exams scored.
- **E14's own handlers** (for models bfcl does not register) are registered in the exam process when needed (`E18_REGISTER_E14`).
