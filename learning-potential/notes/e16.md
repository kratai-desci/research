# E16: LoRA fine-tuning on the job

Nov 17–21, 2026

*First written as a working page; this note is the source of truth from 21 Nov 2026 and adds the results. Shares its design with [E15](e15.md), [E17](e17.md) and [E18](e18.md).*

## Goal

E16 tests whether tiny models learn tool use on the job when their weights change: a LoRA adapter, updated as the model works through the practice tasks. It is the standard way to fine-tune small models and the fine-tuning side of the comparison: notes the model writes itself (E15) against continuous fine-tuning (E16). Everything but the learning method is copied from E15.

## Protocol

| Part | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Models | The six from E14 and E15 |
| Practice, held out, probe | E15's fixed split ([lab/e15/split.json](../data/split.json)): the same 1,200 practice tasks in the same order |
| Oracle | E13's checker, as in E15 |
| Learning | A LoRA adapter, rank 16, alpha 32, learning rate 1e-4, on the attention and MLP projections; the base weights frozen. No extra safeguards; no full fine-tuning |
| Rhythm | Solve one task at a time through vLLM with the current adapter; every 10 tasks, one pass over the new examples plus a replay sample of twice as many earlier ones; the new adapter hot-loaded into vLLM. 120 updates over 1,200 tasks |
| Before | E14's floors; E15's frozen runs (same models, same tasks), reused |
| Seeds | One (15). Qwen3-1.7B's extra seeds were launched and stopped within the hour (17 Nov), for consistency |

## Arms

| Arm | What the adapter trains on |
| --- | --- |
| Oracle teaching | The expected calls of every task, written in the model's own tool-call format (supervised fine-tuning) |
| Oracle judging | Only the model's own attempts that passed, exactly as it wrote them (self-imitation, STaR-style) |

**Teaching targets** are built by replaying the expected calls through the model's own BFCL handler with a stand-in "model" that answers with them: the handler builds exactly the prompts the real model sees (tool results included). Checked on 30 tasks for each of the six models: the replay passes the checker 30 of 30, and every rendered answer decodes back to the expected calls. For Qwen3-1.7B, which reasons in a `<think>` block first, the targets open with an empty think block that the trainer moves into the masked prompt, so they teach the calls without teaching it to stop reasoning. Each turn's scripted closing reply ("Done.") is trained on too: it teaches the model to end a turn.

**Judging** learns nothing for Llama, Falcon and Gemma, which pass no practice task; their runs are kept as a result.

## Runs

12 runs (6 models × 2 arms). Qwen3-1.7B on spot A100s (measured 2.3 times faster than an L4: about 23 s a task with updates); the other models on L4s. After Google reclaimed the five spot L4s (17 Nov, 08:31 UTC) and they sat stopped for 27 hours, the ten unfinished runs resumed from their saved state on regular VMs (six A100s, four L4s), some in Tokyo when Singapore ran out of A100s. Time per task: Qwen3-1.7B 23–24 s; LFM2.5 12–19 s; Llama 13–20 s; Falcon 11–27 s; Gemma 11–45 s; Qwen3.5 78–102 s (slow through the newer vLLM even on an A100).

## Pilot (17 Nov)

Qwen3-1.7B, oracle teaching, on one L4 and one spot A100. It found two bugs before any real run:

1. **Out of memory:** 63 of the first 78 training examples were silently skipped; the full vocabulary scores of a long prompt did not fit. Fixed by scoring only the answer's positions (`logits_to_keep`).
2. **No closing replies:** an attempt to train only on the call steps made the model keep calling and never end a turn (tasks 61–82: 5 solved against frozen's 17). The closing replies went back in.

The corrected pilot showed the dip later seen in the full runs: worse than frozen over tasks 11–100 (27 against 56), then better over 101–200 (53 against 49).

## Measures

As E15: gain over frozen on practice; the probe curve (the adapter saved every 100 tasks, scored on the 200 probe tasks with learning off, [deploy/gcp/run_e16_probe.sh](../src/deploy/gcp/run_e16_probe.sh)); the BFCL exam with the final adapter merged into the weights ([E18](e18.md)).

## Findings

1. **Weight updates are the method that works.** On unseen practice-pool tasks, oracle teaching lifts LFM2.5 from 26 to 65, Qwen3.5 from 25.5 to 68.5, and the three zero-floor models from 0 to 31–58.
2. **Only weights get below the floor.** Llama, Falcon and Gemma solve no practice task frozen, with notes (E15) or with retrieval (E17); taught LoRA brings them to 20–49% of practice tasks and 3–11.5% of BFCL's official multi-turn base.
3. **Judging works where the model already succeeds** (Qwen3.5 +42.5 on the probe, LFM2.5 +22, Qwen3-1.7B +11.5 at the end) and cannot start below the floor (nothing passes, nothing is learnt).
4. **The strongest model is hurt by teaching.** Qwen3-1.7B's taught adapter swings (7, 61, 64, 28, 39 on the probe) and ends below its start; on BFCL it loses 9 points of multi-turn base and 30 of live. Likely cause: the targets skip its reasoning (empty think block), and learning to answer without thinking costs a model that relies on it. Learning from its own verified successes (judging), reasoning included, keeps it and gains (+5 on BFCL).
5. **Learning transfers only partly.** Probe gains of 30–60 points become 3–11.5 points on BFCL's multi-turn base; the official tasks are harder (frozen Qwen3-1.7B: 51% of the probe, 20% of BFCL) and written differently.
6. **Side effects:** taught LoRA lifts single-turn calls for the weak models (Llama live 11.8 → 41.3; Falcon non-live 10.0 → 45.2) but cuts memory to near 0 for every model, and Qwen3.5's judging adapter loses 11.7 points of non-live.

*Seeds 16 and 17 for six of these runs, and teaching with the reasoning kept, are in [E19](e19.md): findings 1 to 3 hold over seeds; finding 4 is revised there (teaching makes Qwen3-1.7B unstable rather than uniformly worse, and keeping its reasoning fixes the instability but not its loss on BFCL's multi-turn tasks).*

## Statistical check (E20)

Tested in [E20](e20.md) (paired McNemar tests, Holm-corrected; E19's seeds included):
- Certain: every taught LoRA run's probe gain (+29 to +58.5), and Llama and Gemma off 0 on BFCL multi-turn base in every seed.
- Not significant after correction: the middle models' BFCL multi-turn base gains (LFM2.5 +3.5 and Qwen3.5 +5.7 over three seeds), Falcon's +3, and Qwen3-1.7B judging's +3.0.
- Qwen3-1.7B teaching: its losses on live and non-live are significant in every seed; on multi-turn base only E16's seed (−9) is, and the three-seed mean (−4.3) is not.

## What happened along the way

- **Spot VMs reclaimed (17 Nov):** five spot L4s stopped and nothing restarted them for 27 hours. The runs resumed from their saved state on regular VMs.
- **Resume bugs (18 Nov):** judging runs with no passed task had no saved adapter and crashed on resume (fixed: resume from the base model); a log line cut off by the lost VM crashed Gemma's judging run (fixed: read logs and examples up to the first unreadable line).
- **Newer-stack LoRA:** vLLM 0.33 loaded LoRA adapters for Qwen3.5 and LFM2.5 at run time, which was the open risk; it worked.
